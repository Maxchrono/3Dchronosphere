Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. The short version

Chronosphere is privacy-first by design. There are no accounts, no passwords, no user profiles, and no databases of personal information. We cannot sell or leak what we never store.

2. Location information

Timezone (no permission required): your browser shares your device's configured IANA timezone (e.g. Asia/Kolkata). We use it only to display your local time and a representative city. It is processed in memory and never stored server-side.

GPS (only with explicit permission): if — and only if — you grant browser location permission, your coordinates are sent once to our server to find the nearest known city, used to position the marker, and then discarded. Coordinates are never logged, stored, or shared.

If you deny or ignore the permission request, the app simply uses your timezone. Everything keeps working.

3. Data stored on your device

The "Save location" feature writes to your browser's localStorage. This data lives only on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

4. Cookies & third parties

Chronosphere itself sets no cookies. Map and place data are sourced from Natural Earth and GeoNames (non-personal, public datasets). If advertising (e.g. Google AdSense) is enabled in the future, third-party cookies will be used only after your consent via a consent banner, as required by GDPR/ePrivacy.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we store no personal data server-side, most requests (access, deletion, portability) are inherently satisfied. For any privacy question or request, contact us at hello.chronosphere@gmail.com.

6. Children's privacy

The service is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. The server applies a strict Content-Security-Policy, rate limiting, and input validation. No sensitive data exists on our infrastructure to protect.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

About Chronosphere

Chronosphere is a global time & location engine: a real-time 3D globe that turns the world's timezones into something you can see, spin, and understand instantly.

What it does

  • Search any of ~200,000 places and see its exact local time, UTC offset and IANA timezone.
  • Live day/night terminator computed from real astronomical time — the shadow sweeps the map as Earth turns.
  • Stylized Sun & Moon environment on deep zoom, positioned by real-world time.
  • Privacy-first local detection: timezone by default, GPS only with your permission.

    • How it works

    Your device timezone resolves first (no permission needed), giving an instant, correct local clock. Optional GPS refines the marker to your nearest city. The globe renders Natural Earth boundaries in WebGL with level-of-detail switching (50m ↔ 10m) for speed.

    Data sources & attribution

    Map boundaries: Natural Earth (public domain). Places & timezones: GeoNames (CC-BY 3.0). Timezone rules: IANA Time Zone Database. Rendering: Three.js (MIT).

What Time Is It at the South Pole?

Chronosphere Editorial Team · Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

At the geographic poles, all longitudes converge, so "solar time" is undefined — and the Sun rises and sets once a year. Time there cannot be astronomical; it must be social.

Time follows the supply chain

Research stations simply adopt the clock of their logistics base. The US Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station and New Zealand's McMurdo Station both run on New Zealand time, because resupply flights stage through Christchurch.

Real IANA zones on ice

Antarctica has genuine IANA zones — Antarctica/McMurdo, Antarctica/Palmer, Antarctica/Troll — and Chronosphere can search its research stations, even though nobody "lives" there permanently.

The terminator goes polar

Around the solstices the day/night terminator hugs the polar circles, giving months of midnight sun or polar night — the most dramatic case of the shadow line you see on our globe.

Search "Amundsen-Scott" on the globe and watch its clock run on New Zealand time at the bottom of the world. Related: the terminator · the date line
